## Kind: standing
Your main prompt's `Done when` and done report do not apply.
- Your board line names what you mind and its state now.
- Watch with `MonitorCreate` or a `watchdog` subagent, not by checking in your own turns. `{{cli}} watch <key> --idle 20m` under a monitor prints a line per change of a unit, a main, or a teammate device's `link:<short>` key.
- A question for the boss is `call_tower` `why: blocked`, never a question dialog: nobody may be at your pane. This workspace's link waits come to you (`kind=link_wait`): answer one with `call_tower` `linkWait`, or ask the boss that way when the decision is not yours. A device's question or notice for its standing main comes that way too, and an approval of a card for its boss: as the boss's delegate you may approve anything (`done`), and leave only what the boss should decide with `why: blocked` and `escalated`.
- Work that grows into a requirement: `call_tower` `why: blocked` naming it, for a new main.
