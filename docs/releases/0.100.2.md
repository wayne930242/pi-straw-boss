# pi-straw-boss 0.100.2

Released 2026-11-26. A patch release on [0.100.1](0.100.1.md). Design record: [2026-11-01-zen-view](../specs/2026-11-01-zen-view.md), Z-D20, R1 amended.

## Changed

- **Zen's terminal layer drifts back to the breath when its step is over.** After a command's own Enter is taken, a pane closes, or an armed terminal's unit works again, the bar says so for 2.5 s, then the layer fades for 1.5 s and Zen breathes again (reduced motion: no fade). A tap, a key, or a scroll in the layer before it is gone keeps it until **Back to Zen**, so a boss who is still using the terminal is never pulled out of it. A terminal opened only to look stays until he leaves.
