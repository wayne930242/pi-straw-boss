# pi-straw-boss 0.105.1

Released 2026-11-26. A patch release on [0.105.0](0.105.0.md).

## Fixed

- **Subagents no longer stop at their first turn.** The doctor's session-start self-check ran in children too and expected every child to have `intercom`, `todo`, `MonitorCreate`, the `subagent*` tools and `subagent_done`. A role's tool list leaves some of them out, and an auto-exit launch has no `subagent_done`, so workers, scouts and reviewers were told to hand over and stopped before their work. A child now gets no self-check message: its tools are its launch's choice and its parent supervises it. Mains, members, trains and towers still get it, and `cli.mjs doctor --session` still diagnoses a child; see [The doctor](../how-it-works.md#the-doctor).
