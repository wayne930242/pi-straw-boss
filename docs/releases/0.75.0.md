# pi-straw-boss 0.75.0

Not yet released.

A session's own files no longer make it a tower or a train: its role record and its saved entry only claim the role, and the tab Herdr lists it in proves it.

## Role proof

Todo #131, from review finding SB4R-F003 (a pre-existing trust in the role record, found while the train's session guard was reviewed).

- **The problem.** A session's role record (`roles/<hash>.json` in the state folder) and the `straw-boss` entries of its session file are files its own shell can write. A member, a main, or a train that wrote `{"role":"tower"}` into its record, or a tower entry into its session file, started as a tower at its next `/reload`, `/resume`, or restore: it took the workspace's `tower` token and the tower's tools, or, beside a live tower, closed its own tab as a yielding tower does. A saved `train` entry or a `train` record did the same for the train role.
- **A record and an entry claim; Herdr proves.** A session is a tower only when its process was started as one (`STRAW_BOSS_ROLE=tower`, or a tower earlier in the same process), or when Herdr lists its pane in a tab labelled with the tower glyph. A train's entry or record counts only in the tab labelled `train <MMDDx>` for its code. The label is what a Herdr restore keeps of a tab Straw Boss made, no file holds it, and the sb server already seats its own towers by it ([spec §29](../specs/2026-11-07-train-workers.md), with the reason the server's word was not chosen).
- **No proof, no role.** A claim Herdr does not prove starts a member (a train: no train), with one warning that names the file and the tab. A tab list Herdr fails to answer proves nothing either way: the start stays unplaced and is asked again, and nothing joins meanwhile.
- **The labels are Straw Boss's own.** `boss_rename` and a main's `label` in `boss_open_main` refuse the tower glyph, the head tower's glyph, and `train <MMDDx>`.
- **The server.** A `roles/` record with role `tower` becomes a tower service in a tower tab, or, in a tab without the glyph, only for a pane that carries the live tower's `sb_role=tower` mark and runs the record's session file. The record and the file alone no longer do: the pi in that pane would have been given a tower run at its next start.
- **A train's start.** A session with a sign of a train whose role is not settled yet (Herdr has not answered for its pane or tab) joins nothing early: `/boss` and a subagent call are refused until it is, where before they could publish a train's pane as a member and promote it.

### What changes for you

- **A tower's session resumed outside a tower tab is a member.** `/resume` or `pi --session` of a tower's session file made a tower in any tab; it now does so only in a tab labelled with the tower glyph.
- **A renamed tab is not proven after a restore.** A resident tower or a train whose tab you renamed away from its label comes back a member after a Herdr restore (a `/reload` of a tower started as one keeps it). The server's own tower seat already had this limit.
- **A main is unchanged.** Nothing outside its files survives a restore for a main (its tab's label is its own name), so its record stands on the checks it had: the newest record of its tab, in a new terminal. A forged main record gives what a member may take by itself, except that at a real restore it can win the tab over the recorded main; that residual is accepted and recorded in the spec.
- **The boundary.** The proof is against files a session can edit. A model that runs Herdr commands as you can rename a tab or open a tower tab itself, which no same-user guard prevents.
