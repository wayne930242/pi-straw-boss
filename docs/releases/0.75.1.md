# pi-straw-boss 0.75.1

Not yet released.

A car's tickets are kept whole: an integrator can receive and return a car with more than 16 tickets.

## Fixes

Todo #154, from a live v3 car (0.74.0) whose slip named 24 tickets: the tower forwarded it, and the integrator's `train_car state=returned` was refused with `sb: car MP-3627 does not match the forwarded slip: tickets differs from what the slip the tower forwarded says`.

- **No silent cut of a car's identity.** The forward record the server keeps of a tower's forward, the manifest's car row and the yard page's car view each cut the car's tickets to the first 16, while the integrator's claim (read from the same slip) carried all of them, so any car with more than 16 tickets could be neither received nor returned. They keep the whole list now, and the forward record and the integrator's claim compare whole lists.
- **One bound for a car's tickets.** A car carries at most 64 tickets, each at most 64 characters (`MAX_CAR_TICKETS` and `MAX_TICKET_CHARS` in `src/train/spec.ts`), the same bound in the slip check, the car file, the evidence file's `car.tickets` and the trainboard. A slip over it is refused by `train check` up front (`sb: slip tickets names 70 tickets, and a car carries at most 64: split the car into two or more slips`), so a sender splits the car instead of finding the refusal at the integrator. The trainboard's per-car cap was 16 and is 64; the board still shortens a car's tickets to fit its size cap only when a board is too big for the writer, as it did before, and nothing compares against that board.
