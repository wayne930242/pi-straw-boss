# pi-straw-boss 0.111.0

Released 2026-11-28. A minor release on [0.110.0](0.110.0.md).

## Added

- **Car rows: where each car is, under its todo.** Between a slip and its landing a coordinator heard four notices and nothing else. Now each session asks the sb server every 15 s for the cars it coordinates or received (`cars-rows`), and answers the probe `straw-boss:car-rows:v1` on `pi.events`, which pi-todo-ledger reads to draw a row under the open todo that names the car's ticket, under Other cars when none does, and an integrator's trains under Trains. A row is the "ladder" the boss chose, with the train's own stage id in every language: `🚂 1127l ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ merge 12m · waits 1127j`. A car that waits shows its departure rule; one that landed, was returned or was dropped shows its version or reason for 30 minutes. No new notice is sent between the four. See [Car rows](../how-it-works.md#car-rows) and the probe's contract, [docs/specs/2026-11-28-car-rows.md](../specs/2026-11-28-car-rows.md).
- 車廂列：每節車廂在哪裡，掛在它的 todo 底下。從送出車廂單到落地，協調者以前只聽到四則通知。現在每個 session 每 15 秒向 sb server 問一次它協調或收車的車廂（`cars-rows`），並在 `pi.events` 上回答探針 `straw-boss:car-rows:v1`；pi-todo-ledger 讀它，把一列畫在標題寫著該車廂單號的未完成 todo 底下，找不到就畫在「其他車廂」，調度員的火車畫在「火車」。一列就是 boss 選的「梯子」，關卡在任何語言都用火車自己的 id：`🚂 1127l ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ merge 12m · waits 1127j`。等發車的車廂顯示發車規則；落地、退回或剔除的車廂顯示版本號或原因，留 30 分鐘。四則通知之間不加任何新通知。
- **`/boss cars`** prints the same rows, with their times, grouped under their todos, Other cars and Trains.
- `/boss cars` 印出同樣的列和時間，依 todo、「其他車廂」、「火車」分組。
- **Car cards in the GUI.** A main's unit card shows its cars, each with its ladder or phase, its time and what it waits on, and `See this train ›`, which opens the yard scrolled to that train and marks it.
- GUI 的車廂小卡。主代理的單位卡顯示它的車廂，每張有梯子或階段、時間、在等什麼，以及「看這班火車 ›」，點了開調車場並捲到那班火車、加上標示。
- **One reminder for a slip nobody received.** A slip sent with `call_tower` (`why: "shared"`) shows as `📮 sent` until the server holds its car. When no integrator has received it 15 minutes after it was sent, sb tells the session's model once: ask the integrator, or send the slip again so the tower forwards it again.
- 車廂單沒人收時提醒一次。用 `call_tower`（`why: "shared"`）送出的車廂單，在 server 持有那節車廂之前顯示為 `📮 已送出`。送出 15 分鐘還沒有調度員收車，sb 會提醒 session 的模型一次：問調度員，或重送車廂單讓塔台再轉一次。

## Compatibility

- Slips, notices, car states and trainboards are unchanged, so a box on an older sb keeps working with this one: an older ledger draws no rows, an older sb answers no probe. The rows of a car need the sb server of the computer that runs its train; a car whose train runs on another box shows no row.
- 車廂單、通知、車廂狀態與火車看板都沒有改，所以停在舊版 sb 的機器照常互通：舊的 ledger 不畫列，舊的 sb 不回答探針。車廂列要靠跑那班火車的那台電腦的 sb server；火車在另一台機器上跑的車廂不會有列。
