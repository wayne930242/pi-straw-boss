# pi-straw-boss 0.93.0

Released 2026-11-26. A minor release on [0.92.0](0.92.0.md). Design records: [2026-11-26-standing-prompt.md](../specs/2026-11-26-standing-prompt.md), [2026-11-26-rotation-close.md](../specs/2026-11-26-rotation-close.md).

## Added

- **A standing main runs its own prompt.** `prompts/standing.md` replaces `main.md` plus `duty-standing.md` for a standing main (boss, 2026-11-26: 「無人塔台值守的 prompt 不該是 main 的，應該要量身訂做」), about 2,650 characters instead of 6,092. It keeps watching, link waits and delegate approvals, capacity incidents, relaying ownerless notices to their owner, dispatching a queue a project rule or handoff gives it, and merging or deploying only on the boss's words.
- **A standing main with nothing armed is woken.** Idle 2 hours with no watch (including `link:` keys), monitor, loop (including cron) or subagent, it gets one message to re-arm, under auto mode too. It never gets the 30-minute wrap-up nudge.
- **Re-arm text at the moment it is needed.** A standing main that is revived, resumed after a Herdr restart, or a handover successor is told to re-arm its watches, monitors and loops, and that a retired monitor or loop is not finished.
- **A rotated-out main closes itself.** Once its successor confirms the takeover and nothing of it runs, the old main clears its board and closes itself and its pane, under auto mode too. A subagent result that arrives meanwhile is forwarded to the successor. A failed read of its lists keeps it open.
- **A note on a wait.** `call_tower` `linkWait` `note` sends a step on a teammate device's wait without closing it; the receiver tells the main that raised the wait, else a standing main, else the tower.

## Fixed

- **No ask or stale mark for a working pane** (boss, 2026-11-26: 「herdr state 還在 working 的 agent 不必被詢問」). `stale`, the main's own copy of `under`, the Auto watch's idle list, and the tower's "ask it to dispatch" skip a main whose Herdr pane is working.
- **A `link:<device>` watch wakes on content only**, not on a board refresh or a tower run.
- **A teammate device's paused main** raises a `quota` notice to this device's standing main and the head tower, once per device and entry, and `resumed` when it clears.

## Changed

- Refactor with no behavior change: one activity probe (`src/stillruns.ts`), one idle-wake mechanism (`src/idlewake.ts`), `boss_wrap` and `call_tower` split into named steps, the boss-question relay in `src/bossrelay.ts`, and the moment texts in `src/moments.ts`.
