# pi-straw-boss 0.103.0

Released 2026-11-26. A minor release on [0.102.0](0.102.0.md).

## Added

- **`call_peer` reaches every peer sb can name at the call.** Besides the tab's main, `to` now takes `sender` (the session whose intercom message is newest on your branch), `caller` (who opened you: a successor's old main until it took over, else the opener's mark; the tower for an integrator it opened, the integrator for a train), `main` with `of` (another live main by exact name, todo item key `t_…` or `<session8>#<id>`, or tab id or label), and `integrator` (the one live integrator of your checkout's project). A target whose role record says it handed over is reached at its successor. No target, or several matches, is refused with what was found (up to five candidates); a main asking for `main` without `of`, a teammate's device as caller, and a session nobody opened are refused too. Measured over 14 days, 73% of sb sends went to a session that had just written to the sender, and 24% of sends to a new target failed, most of them on a remembered name.
- **Mains of every kind and trains carry `call_peer`**, not only members. The tool is 444 bytes (member +146 over 0.102.0; main and train +444, 435 characters by the tool budget's count). Towers keep `tower_send` and `tower_reply`; subagents have no sb tools.

## Changed

- The prompts that told a session to use raw intercom now name `call_peer`: an opened main reports to `caller`, a successor tells its old main it took over with `caller` (sb counts that call as the takeover, as it does an intercom send), a member refused promotion tells the main with `to: main` (in a train's tab, the tower with `call_tower`), a main answers a car slip under integration off with `sender`, and a standing main forwards a notice to its owner with `main` and `of`. Raw intercom stays for sessions outside the sb net and for diagnostics. A subagent's report to its launcher (cut and orphan notices) stays on intercom, since a subagent has no sb tools.
