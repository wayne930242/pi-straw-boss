# pi-straw-boss 0.87.0

Released 2026-11-24. A minor release on [0.86.1](0.86.1.md).

## Added

- **Supply board.** The head page's board draws device capacity as a supply tray: one slot per budget point, each deployed unit a chip over its points, free slots that pulse only while the device is under capacity, and a barracks with the ready work. The fold adds mini slots; each main shows its units and ready count; teammate devices are listed. With no budget only the deployed units are drawn. Standard renames the board 戰況板 with 指揮官 and 詢問戰況; Raven Keep draws candle graves, bats at the keep's gate, and a coffin for a quota pause; Geek stays plain text; Zen shows no capacity ([spec](../specs/2026-11-24-capacity-supply/spec.md)). Unit names stay on this computer's page: `board_read`, the link, and the operate slice carry none.

## Changed

- **Sound only when you are needed.** A sound effect plays only for what waits on the boss: a question, a pending dialog, a trust prompt, and link notices that need an answer. Finishes, failures, the deploy panel's "unit ready", and the supply board are silent; their system notification and on-page notice stay. The done and error clips are removed.
- **Raven Keep's sounds.** The question bell, the background rain, and the distant thunder are recorded clips from Casper's library, in place of the old bell and the synthesized weather. The Background switch, the volume, and every weather rule are unchanged. These clips are not MIT; see `NOTICE.md`.
- **An answered wait card folds.** On the tower card, its comms, and the head page, a wait card folds to one line once the server takes your choice ("You chose: … · waiting for the tower to confirm"), also after a reload. It still opens to skip or reply until the tower resolves it. Starting secret entry keeps the card open for the second step.

## Fixed

- A parked wait card in the auto-mode return panel no longer freezes after it is pressed; it follows the snapshot until the tower ends it.
- On the board, a stale main's status icon and word stay on one line.
