# pi-straw-boss 0.98.0

Released 2026-11-26. A minor release on [0.97.0](0.97.0.md). Design record: [2026-11-26-storybook-theme](../specs/2026-11-26-storybook-theme/spec.md).

## Added

- **Storybook view (繪本).** A new GUI view, chosen in the settings or with `/boss gui view storybook`: a pressed-flower notebook skin of Standard with a day look (06:00 to 17:59) and a night look, switched by the device's clock. The map and the head tower are drawn as an apple-tree house by day and by night, with clouds, butterflies and a falling leaf by day, stars, lamplight and fireflies by night (still under calm, reduced motion and forced colours). States show as hand-drawn figures: a pencil, an envelope, a flag and a moon. Roles have woodcut animal icons and the tab a bunny favicon. A question plays a kalimba note, and with background sound on, the map and the head page play birdsong by day and crickets by night. The pictures are Codex image generation; the sounds are Casper's and not MIT (NOTICE.md).
- **Unit kinds in Storybook and Raven Keep.** Both views draw their own icons for the seven kinds (coordinator, integrator, standing, worker, scout, planner, reviewer) instead of falling back to Standard's.
- **Grouped and multiple review picks.** The GUI's block host takes pi-better-show 0.10.1: a `review` block can ask for one pick per group or any number of picks, a pick can be made in the lightbox, and a grouped pick keeps its full group name when the page reloads.

## Changed

- **Zen is a mode, not a view.** Every theme's header has a Zen button (an ensō on a phone, `[Zen]` in Geek); leaving Zen returns to the device's own theme (Raven Keep, Geek, Storybook or Standard) instead of Standard. The device keeps the mode as `sb.zen`; a device that stored Zen as its view stays in Zen, also after it picks a theme. `/boss gui view zen` is refused with the way in, and a stored `zen` view reads as `auto`.
- **Standard's question clip is a marimba note** from Casper's sound effects (not MIT; NOTICE.md), replacing the old waiting chime.
