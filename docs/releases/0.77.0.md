# pi-straw-boss 0.77.0

Released 2026-11-12. The merge train asks for skills that declare the merge-train contract instead of one package, and three car lifecycle bugs found by the car-forward model are fixed.

## Changes

- **The merge-train contract.** The train no longer checks the version of the pi-anchorloop package. It needs the `integrating` and `deploying` skills pi loaded to declare `merge-train-contract: "1"` under `metadata:` in their frontmatter; a missing skill, a missing declaration, or another version refuses a train's and the integrator's mutating `todo` and `MonitorCreate` calls with the reason, before anything changes. Until pi-anchorloop ships the declaration, an undeclared skill inside pi-anchorloop 2.11.0 or newer counts as contract 1. See [the merge-train contract](/pi-straw-boss/docs/guide/merge-train/).
- **A car re-sent after an end starts a new life** (#14). A forward newer than a car's `returned` or `dropped` state is received again, and its notices go out as usual. The earlier life stays in the book.
- **A car lands once** (#15). `received` is refused when the car's head is already on the base's remote branch, checked with git and nothing the server remembers, and when the app names no usable base. A car merged by squash or rebase is not detected.

## Fixes

- **A returned car's notice is confirmed** (#13). An integrator that returns a car waits until the notice reaches the car's coordinator, and calls its tower `blocked` when the sb server no longer holds the car (for example after a restart), when the notice is not delivered within a day, or when the session ends first.
- **A notice in flight stays with its own life.** A send's result is recorded on the life it was sent for, not on a newer life of the same car.
- **Receipts no longer hold the car book.** The base check of `received` runs before the one-at-a-time queue, so a train's `landed` does not wait behind slow fetches.
- **Dismiss finished takes ally boxes' finished subagents too.** The HUD button counted them but hid only this computer's; it now dismisses each one, and counts only what it can hide.
- **Raven Keep's rain keeps falling under calm on a desktop.** On a fine pointer the rain no longer stops after 30 s without input, as the thunder already did; on a phone it still rests.
