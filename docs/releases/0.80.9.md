# pi-straw-boss 0.80.9

Released 2026-11-17. A fix release on [0.80.8](0.80.8.md).

## Fixes

- **A late wait notice is held 25 min, longer than a round of four cards,** so a stopped turn's first press no longer loses its `wait_card` to a slow round.
- **Only the raiser's own call clears or drops a held notice;** a `wait-notice` from another asker leaves it.
- **A stop that lands during the clear request** no longer drops both the tool result and the notice: the notice is recovered and sent.
