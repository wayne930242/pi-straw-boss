Two more wording fixes from the prompt audit. No behavior changes.

No train in this computer's ledgers lacks a trainboard unfinished (every one is landed, superseded, or ended). The line goes, with the clauses that served only that path: the integrator's own go/wait in `tower-run.md` and `tower.md`, and "a deploy the integrator starts itself is `why=shared`" in `main.md`. The "Moving a project to 0.65.0" guide says such a train is history.
