# pi-straw-boss 0.92.0

Released 2026-11-26. A minor release on [0.91.0](0.91.0.md). Design record: [2026-11-26-just-in-time-prompts.md](../specs/2026-11-26-just-in-time-prompts.md).

## Changed

- **A main's prompt holds only what every turn needs.** Rules needed at one moment now arrive at that moment (boss, 2026-11-25: 「提示詞可以逐步交付」). Rendered `main.md` went from 5,608 to about 4,160 characters with integration on, and from 3,847 to about 2,480 with it off.
  - The capacity notice sent to the main whose work it is says to dispatch now or say why on the board, and to reply to whoever asked.
  - `call_tower`'s description carries the `shared` and `blocked` details. A `blocked` result says the answer comes as a `wait_card` to act on. A `shared` call that carries a car slip is told it waits for sb's car notice.
  - `board_set` and `boss_duty` descriptions carry their own rules; `boss_pair` keeps one pointer in `main.md`.
  - Every car notice, including the tower's relay when the coordinator could not be told, ends with its next step: rework and resend when returned, wait when received, complete the todo when landed.
  - With integration off, a car slip that reaches a main gets a note to answer its sender that integration is off here.
  - "Idle with ready todos: dispatch them." stays in `main.md`: under auto mode there is no idle nudge.
- **`npm test` checks the zh-TW twin of how-it-works** (`tests/twin.test.ts`): line-for-line kinds and backticked tokens, headings, and bold, which before failed only the Pages deploy after a push. Untranslated commits stay a warning.

## CI

- The publish gate runs the full suite in four parallel shards.
