# pi-straw-boss 0.80.8

Released 2026-11-16. A feature release on [0.80.7](0.80.7.md).

## Wait cards, one after another

- **The idle tower pane shows the open wait cards one after another** (card view). The boss presses each in the pane; the next card follows the press.
- **The wake is held while the boss answers.** While the prompt's polls say he is answering, no run of that tower starts, whatever waits, so a press does not take the pane from under the next card. The hold ends 10 s after the last such poll, at once when the prompt says he is done, and 5 min after the first at the latest; then one run takes everything. A held wake begins no run before its own time (a milestone's wait) once the hold lapsed, and an unattended device holds none.
- **`/boss wait` goes to the next card** with a **Next card** choice, and the list comes back for the way back.
- **`boss_wait_ask`** has sb draw open wait cards for the boss in the terminal, one after another: a main its own, a local run its tower's, the head's run any. The model names cards and words nothing. The first answer wins everywhere; a card left 60 s without a choice ends as no answer (a reason or reply being typed is not cut off; 5 min is the most for one card), and a card left unanswered is not drawn again for 10 min. A run draws it only while the boss's last words came from its pane in the last 10 min. A main whose turn was stopped after the press still gets its `wait_card`.
- **The rule changed:** a session re-asks a wait card only with `boss_wait_ask`, never `ask_user_question`.
- **Door fixes:** words written at the head's own pane keep the boss at it, and a reply to another tower's card names no door.

## Known limits

- On win32 the card view is off; the numbered picker stays.
- A paired device's digit key can engage the card view (R-F004).
- Held events, trains included, wait up to 5 min while the boss answers.
- A server restart forgets held late notices (a stopped turn's `wait_card`).
