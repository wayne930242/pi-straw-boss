# pi-straw-boss 0.106.0

Released 2026-11-26. A minor release on [0.105.1](0.105.1.md).

## Changed

- **Raven's head page paints far less, and looks the same.** The castle crossfades between its dark and lit paintings when lightning strikes; the window lights, fog, bats and flying raven move or fade without redrawing the scenery; the perched raven's eye glint is a small fixed image instead of a gradient redrawn every frame. In three 10 s Chrome traces the head page painted 18–21 times (Standard 9, before about 440), and its paint plus document pre-paint time is 47–52 % of 0.105's. Only a few pixels around the perched raven's eye differ. Lightning and thunder keep their clock; calm and reduced motion behave as before.
- **Theme contract**: every Raven keyframe now animates only transforms and opacity (the head scene's exception is gone). The head page is measured by paints and paint time (`scripts/paint-trace.mjs BASE OUT raven head ORIGINAL_BASE`), not by document pre-paint counts: Chrome re-checks `content-visibility: auto` rows on every frame in which anything moves, so those counts rise with any smooth motion without repainting.
