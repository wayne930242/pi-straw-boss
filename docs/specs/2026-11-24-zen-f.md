# Zen under headless runs: run cards, live text, the target picker

> **As built (2026-11-24, `feat/headless-towers` after `84f77b3e`).** Everything below was built as written, with the boss's decisions of §9 (ZF-1 to ZF-9, option a). Where the build differs in shape:
>
> - The target button and its list are one module, `gui/targetpicker.ts`, used by Zen (strip and addressed companion) and by the head page; §7.9's `zentarget.ts` is that module. `targetsOf(net, addressed, headLabel)` is in `gui/targets.ts`, with `pickable(net)` (the head's `service.headless`).
> - §7.5's `runAlerts` is `withRuns(alerts, net, dialogs, at)` in `gui/cardalerts.ts`: it adds the run cards to the alert map and marks a tagged wait `asked`.
> - §7.11: `companionOf` is unchanged; the addressed tower is the layer's own state (`zen.ts` `addressed`, `addressedOf`), with the stand-in key `addr:<workspace>` while the tower has no unit.
> - §7.2: the run tells the server through a run-token request, `run-wait-shown {wait}` (runmode.ts, `WaitAskDeps.shown`), before it draws the card and again with none when the card ends; runkids.ts tags that run's dialogs meanwhile.
> - §7.4 (ZF-2): runkids.ts looks at a waiting dialog every 60 s and cancels it when no page of this computer is open (`KidsPort.pages`, the GUI's count of local streams), or at 10 minutes from when it came.
> - The run card's ended words are the model's outcomes `ended` and `expired` (zenmodel.ts `endOf`): a run no longer live, or a dialog that waited its full 10 minutes. Neither rings.
> - With the switch off nothing of this is drawn or sent: the head's `service.headless` is false, so no button; the server's dialogs list is empty, so no `run-dialogs` event; the quiet live text only differs while a run writes, which never happens.
> - §8.3's "under the terminal" arrival and §8.6's "scrolled back" check have no test of their own (the existing terminal-layer and follow tests cover the rules they rest on); §8.10's live check was not run in this build.

## 1. Today at `fb807eea` (read)

- Run dialogs reach a page only inside the history stream of the watched key: `watchSession(..., {local})` sends `dialogs` for a tower key (that workspace's) or the head key (every run's), and only to a local stream (`src/watch.ts`, `src/gui.ts` `runSide`). Zen watches the head's key on the circle, the companion's key on the companion, and nothing or a card's context key while a card shows (`gui/zen.ts` `deps.watch`). **So Zen sees run dialogs only while it rests on the circle, and none are items today.**
- `RunDialog` is `{key, workspace, method, title, message?, options?, approval?}` (`src/net.ts`), with no time, no run kind, and nothing that says it renders a wait card. Non-approval dialogs are cancelled after 60 s, approvals after 10 minutes (`src/server/runkids.ts`).
- Standard draws them above the composer of the tower's page and the head page (`gui/rundialogs.ts` `fillRunDialogs`), answering `POST api/tower/<ws>/dialog` or `api/head/dialog` with `door: page`.
- `live` (the run's message under way) is sent on every page for a tower or head key, at the net reading's pace (`pollMs`, 2 s), and `chat.ts` draws it after the log's last entry with a "Writing now" tag and `aria-live="polite"`.
- The head page's composer parses `@<workspace>` (`gui/headline.ts`) when `unit.head && unit.headless` and sends `to`. **Zen's strip posts `input {text, as}` itself** (`gui/zenhead.ts` `send`) and parses nothing; its sent line waits in `pending` until the head's history brings a copy, which a direct line never would.
- `boss_wait_ask` in a run draws the wait card through `ctx.ui.select`; under RPC that select becomes a `RunDialog` like any other (`src/runmode.ts`, `src/waitask.ts`). The same wait is already a Zen `wait` item (`gui/waits.ts`).
- `companionOf` (`gui/zenmodel.ts`) lands only on a unit the net lists with control; a workspace whose tower has no service has no unit to land on.

## 2. Run dialogs and approval cards in Zen

### 2.1 Which, and where

- **Items:** every dialog a headless run waits on: a `link_request` approval, an ally run's gated call, pi's project-trust question before a run claims, and another extension's `select`, `confirm`, or `input`. A head run's dialogs too.
- **This computer's page only.** The server sends them to local streams alone, as it does today; a paired device's Zen never hears of them: no card, no count in its cue, no reminder. Two pages of the same boss may therefore count differently; that is the authority line (`NO_RELAY`), not a bug.
- **Not a second card for a wait.** A `select` that `boss_wait_ask` draws shows a wait card that is already an item. The server tags such a dialog with its wait (`wait: {workspace, id}`); Zen drops the dialog and marks the wait item *asked*. An asked wait ranks **blocking** (a run's turn now holds on it) while the dialog is open; answering the wait card (Handle, Dismiss) resolves both, since `boss_wait_ask` reads the list every 2 s and closes its dialog when the card is pressed elsewhere.

### 2.2 Order

The table under *Blocking first* (Z-D18) gains one row:

| Kind of item | Rank | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A dialog a headless run waits on (`card.kind` `run`), approvals included; a wait that a run's `boss_wait_ask` shows now | **Blocking** | The run's turn is the tool call that waits for the answer; an approval falls to no after 10 minutes. |

Within blocking, the one that has waited longest first (the dialog's `at`, which the server adds). Everything else is Z-D19 to Z-D22 unchanged: **a run card never takes the screen by itself**, on the circle, a companion, the history, or under the terminal; it queues silently (no bowl, no motion, no change on the page); the cue counts it; Enter or a tap on the cue takes the next; after the boss's own accepted answer the next queued card follows at once (Z-D22); Maybe later and a card that ended return to the breath. A system reminder once per card while the page is hidden or unfocused, as for every card.

### 2.3 What the card shows

One card, Zen's card in ink, at most 40 rem, the whole width on a phone:

- **Who:** the tower in the page's language and its base label (「知識庫 · 塔台」); 「主塔台」 for a head run; for an ally run's gated call, the teammate's device and the workspace (「merlin-box 的隊友執行 · 知識庫」).
- **Title:** 「{name} 要你核准」 for an approval, 「{name} 想問你」 otherwise (the polite live region says the same once).
- **What it asks:** the dialog's title in serif paper; its message under it. An approval's arguments are the confirm card's style: dim monospace on `--zen-tint`, no box, every argument shown (the card scrolls inside itself, the send row pinned).
- **What it said:** the tower's last finished line (`intel` of the tower's key), and the context fold (**H**, 前情) opens the tower's log, as a unit's card does.
- **The answer, by method:**
  - approval or `confirm`: **Allow** / **Deny** (approval), **Yes** / **No** (other);
  - `select`: the options as Zen's choice rows (a hollow dot, filled when chosen), then **Send**;
  - `input`: a hairline field with **Send**; Enter in the field sends, Shift+Enter is a new line, the IME's Enter is the IME's.
  - Every card: **Cancel** (the run gets `cancelled`; for an approval it is Deny) and **Maybe later**.
- **Enter and Esc** (Z-D20): taking the card puts the focus on its primary control (Allow, Yes, the first option, the field), and the next fresh Enter presses it. An approval card also takes the confirm bar's 400 ms dwell (ZNR-F007): an Enter whose keydown comes within 400 ms of the card being drawn, a repeating one, or one that began before it, presses nothing. One Esc cancels (the card's Cancel), as for every cancellable card.
- **Its outcome** goes through the shared reading (`outcome`, `gui/asks.ts`): accepted is the knock, the outcome line for 1.2 s, then Z-D22's next card or the pause; a refusal is the low bowl and the server's words under the card with the buttons back.
- **When it ends under the boss's eyes** (Z-D20): another door answered it (the viewer, another tab), the run ended, or it timed out. The card stays, every answer off, with 「已經在別處回好了」, 「這個執行已結束」 (*The run has ended*), or 「時間到，執行已不再等」 (*Timed out; the run moved on*), and its one button *Back to the breath*. The knock rings only for *answered elsewhere*; an end or a timeout rings nothing, as *This unit has left* does.
- **One send path.** Zen and Standard call one function, `runDialogActions(d)` (moved out of `rundialogs.ts` into `gui/asks.ts`), so the body (`key`, `confirmed`, `value`, `cancelled`) and the route are the same in every view.

## 3. `live` text

- **Where it shows:** only where that session is the screen: the companion when it is an addressed tower (section 5), and the head's whole conversation (history). Never on the strip under the circle (its column of whole lines would reflow every 2 s, which is motion), never on a card, never on the circle. The strip keeps its one working line, 「主塔台處理中…」, and shows the head's reply when the turn's text is final, as today.
- **How:** Zen passes `live: "quiet"` to `mountChat`: the message under way sits after the log's last entry as ash serif text, with no "Writing now" tag, no caret, no animation or transition, and **no `aria-live`** (a region that grows every 2 s is read again and again). It changes at the server's pace (one net reading, 2 s), not per token. The log follows it only while the boss is at the bottom and not touching (the existing follow rule); scrolled back, nothing moves and the log's own "new" pill is the only sign. When the session file brings the message, the entry replaces the live text in place (paper), with no fade.
- **Reduced motion, forced colours:** nothing to change (it has no motion); in forced colours it is `CanvasText`.
- The composer's activity line (`liveNote`, 「工作中」) stays as it is: words, not motion.

## 4. Parked notices and other towers' run lines

- **Parked notices** (FE-1) are a head ask's state on Standard's head page (`parked at <label>`) and a count in the head's brief. Zen shows nothing: no card, no cue, no strip line, no history marker. A parked notice holds no one, and the head's own brief keeps it from being forgotten.
- **Run start and end lines** (`↳ 知識庫 run`, `[知識庫] …`, `✓ done`, `✗ crashed`) are the head *viewer's* ring lines, not history items; Zen reads history only, so they do not reach the strip and must not be added there. The addressed tower's companion shows its own conversation (which is its runs' messages) and its composer's activity line says 工作中 while a run works; a crash or a hold is the composer's existing bar words.
- The strip's existing quiet notes for the head's own forwards (「→ MP Center tower」, 「MP Center tower answered」) are history and stay.

## 5. The target picker and the companion

### 5.1 The button

- **Where:** at the start of the unfolded head input (the strip's box), of the head's whole-conversation composer, and of an addressed tower's companion composer. Not on a main's or member's companion (they are their own conversation), not on the folded hint, not while the strip rests: at rest the circle screen is unchanged.
- **Only with headless runs** (`unit.headless`), as the head page's `@` parsing today; without them the server refuses `to`, and the button is not drawn.
- **Look:** the target's name and a small chevron, 「主塔台 ⌄」, in the small ash sans of names and hints (rule 6), sharing the input's hairline, with a 1 px ash rule between it and the box. No fill, no pill, no frame. Hover brightens it to paper; keyboard focus puts the vermilion line under the word (rule 4). 44 × 44 px at least; on a phone at most 7 em wide, cut with an ellipsis, the whole name in its `aria-label` (「對象：主塔台。換一個」).
- **Tab order:** the button, then the box. Shift+Tab from the box reaches it.

### 5.2 The list

- **Opens** on a click, a tap, Enter or Space on the button, or Alt+↓ in the box. It is Zen's overlay (rule 5): `--zen-veil` with its blur over the page, the list floating on the ink with no panel. On a desktop it rises from the button's left edge (the input is at the foot); on a phone it is a column the width of the screen rising from above the input, and the keyboard is put away while it is open.
- **Rows** (44 px): 主塔台 first; then the towers addressed in this visit, newest first; then every other base on this computer in the map's order (a sub workspace's parent once; a box's bases shown through an operate session are not targets: `@` resolves this computer's workspaces). Each row is the label in paper, the chosen one with a filled dot, the others hollow. One ash word at the right only when it helps: 「對話中」 (the boss's conversation there is live) or 「工作中」 (a run works). **No count of any kind**: no cards per tower, no queue size, no unread.
- **Filter:** a hairline field at the list's top, 「找…」, focused on open on a desktop (typing narrows by every word, as the palette does), not focused on a phone (it would raise the keyboard). Shown on a phone only when there are more than eight targets.
- **Keys:** ↑ ↓ move, Enter picks, Esc closes with nothing changed and the focus back on the button (a new first row of `escStep`, `pick`, before the corner menu). A tap on the veil closes. Picking takes effect at once (5.3).
- **Calm:** the list fades in and out by opacity in 0.3 s (none under reduced motion); nothing slides. It makes no sound.

### 5.3 What a pick does: the companion follows

- **Picking a tower** makes it the companion at once: the circle screen gives way to that tower's conversation (its log, live text, composer), and the draft in the strip's box moves into the companion's box with the focus. The header reads the tower's name and 「直接對話 · <base label>」, so the screen, not a marker on a line, says where the words go; the composer's button reads 「知識庫 ⌄」 and its placeholder 「寫給知識庫塔台」.
- **A typed `@<ws> <text>`** in the strip (or the history's composer) is read as the head page reads it (`headLine`: the first word, `@@` for a literal `@`); it posts `input {text, to}` on the head's route, is **not** added to the strip's pending lines, and once the server accepts it (2xx) the companion becomes that tower, so its answer is on the screen. A refusal stays on the strip with the server's words in clay, the low bowl, and the draft back, as today.
- **A tower with no service yet** is still a target (`@` opens it). Its companion shows the circle faint behind one ash line, 「這裡還沒有塔台；第一句話會叫醒它」, and the composer. While the tower has no unit, the companion's composer posts through the head's route with `to` (the server resolves the workspace and ensures the service with no tab); once the snapshot lists the tower, its log mounts. One path, the server's: the page never opens a service itself.
- **How long:** the rest of the visit (Z-D16): the addressed tower is the companion until the boss picks another target, picks 主塔台, presses Esc, or uses *Back to the breath*, `[`/`]`, or the palette. Zen hiding forgets it; the next visit starts at the breath with the head as the target. Nothing is stored on the device.
- **Back to the head:** the picker's 主塔台 returns to the circle with the strip awake and the input unfolded (he chose to talk to the head). Esc on the addressed companion with nothing to fold or blur returns to the circle with the strip at rest (a new `escStep` row `back`, before the hint and leave: he came here by his own pick, so Esc goes back the way he came, not out of Zen). *Back to the breath* in the palette does the same.
- **Cards while there:** a run dialog of the tower he is talking to is a card like any other: the cue sits under the companion's name (Z-D20) and the screen does not change until he takes it.

## 6. Standard and Geek

- The head page's composer gets the same button before its `+` (`mountComposer` option `target`), with a popover list (`.menu-pop`, the head menu's pattern; a sheet from the foot on a phone): 主塔台, then the bases, label and the same optional state word, no counts.

## 10. Risks

| Risk | Guard |
|---|---|
| A line goes to a tower he did not mean | The screen is the tower's (name, 直接對話, its log), the button names it, Esc returns to the breath, and the target dies with the visit. |
| A run card buried behind older non-blocking cards | It ranks blocking; within blocking it is ordered by age. |
| Run cards expire before he takes them | ZF-2; and a card that ends on the screen says why instead of vanishing. |
| Approval pressed by a stray Enter | Z-D20's held-key guard plus the 400 ms dwell, and a fresh press only. |
| A paired device's Zen looks calm while an approval waits at the computer | By design (`NO_RELAY`); the computer's page reminds once per card. |
| The picker becomes a dashboard (counts, unread) | Rows carry a label and at most one state word; the no-count test scans them. |
