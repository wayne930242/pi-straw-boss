# Car rows: where a car is, under its todo (2026-11-28)

Status: implemented on `feat/car-rows` for 0.111.0 (sb's side: batches 1, 3 and 4 of the design). Design and the boss's choices: `scratch/trainview/design.md` of weihung-agent-root (boss form, 2026-11-28). pi-todo-ledger draws the rows (batch 2) from the probe below; this file is the contract it reads.

## Why

A coordinator that sends a car slip hears four notices (received, landed, returned, dropped) and nothing between them: over 105 cars since 11/25 the median gap from receipt to the end was 74 minutes, and one car of train 2026-11-26-zj went 9.5 hours with two notices. Before receipt it hears nothing at all, and a slip lost in an sb or tower restart sat unseen for two hours (11/07). The boss chose:

- **Row lifetime**: a row shows while its car is on its way, and for 30 minutes after it lands, is returned or is dropped.
- **No mid-train notices**: the row is the progress; no new intercom message wakes the coordinator between the four notices.
- **One reminder**: a slip that no integrator received 15 minutes after it was sent is told to its coordinator once.
- **Wording "ladder"**: `🚂 1127l ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ 合併 12m`, with what the train waits on after it when there is one.

Nothing of the slip, the notices, the car states or the trainboards changes, so a box still on an older sb keeps working with this one: an older ledger draws no rows, and an older sb answers no probe.

## The probe: `straw-boss:car-rows:v1`

On `pi.events`, as `pi-herdr-agents:run-rows:v1` is: the reader emits a plain object and sb's handler, synchronous, fills `rows` on it. sb answers in every pi in a Herdr pane that is no subagent; a session with no cars answers `{ version: 1, rows: [] }`.

```ts
// pi.events.emit("straw-boss:car-rows:v1", probe); afterwards probe.rows is set, or absent when sb is not loaded.
interface CarRowsProbe { rows?: { version: 1; rows: CarRowV1[] } }
interface CarRowV1 {
  id: string;        // stable for one car's life: "<project>/<app>-<head12>#<life>"; "sent:<app>-<head12>" before receipt; "train:<project>/<code>" for an integrator's train
  item?: string;     // the todo the row goes under: its uid (t_ and 10 base32 letters), else "<session8>#<id>"
  group?: string;    // set exactly when item is absent: the heading in the session's language, "Other cars"/"其他車廂" or "Trains"/"火車"
  tickets: string[]; // the car's tickets; for a train row, every car's
  phase: "sent" | "waiting" | "riding" | "landed" | "returned" | "dropped";
  tone: "ok" | "wait" | "warn" | "bad";
  line: string;      // the words before the time, in the session's language, plain text
  wait?: string;     // the words after the time: what the car waits on, the reason of a return, the ticket of a row with no item
  since: number;     // ms since the epoch: when the car's current stage began
}
```

A reader draws one row as `${line} ${duration(now - since)}${wait ? " · " + wait : ""}`, with `duration` as the worker rows have it (`42s`, `12m`, `1h05m`), and colours it by `tone`. It needs no word of the trains: sb writes every word. A reader that finds `rows.version` other than 1, or a row whose `id`, `line` or `phase` is missing or whose `since` is not finite, skips that row (or all of them), as it does for run rows.

What each phase says (zh-TW, en):

| phase | line | wait | tone |
|---|---|---|---|
| sent | `📮 已送出` / `📮 sent` | `調度員還沒收` / `not received yet` | wait; warn from 15 min |
| waiting | `🚉 FE2 等發車` / `🚉 FE2 waiting to depart` | the departure rule, `滿 4 節或 60 分（還 23 分）` | wait |
| riding | `🚂 1127l ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ 合併` | what the step waits on, `等 1127j 合併道（FE2）`; `火車停滯` when the train stalled | ok; wait while it waits; warn when stalled |
| landed | `✅ 1127l 已落地 1.62.0-beta.24` | | ok |
| returned | `↩ 退回` / `↩ returned` | the first line of the reason | bad |
| dropped | `✖ 1127l 剔除` / `✖ 1127l dropped` | the first line of the reason | bad |

The ladder is the stages of the app's last train that landed (the kinds of its last round, in order: FE2's assemble, review, full check, merge, CI, deploy verify, land are 7 cells). The current stage's cells before it are full (`▰`), the rest empty (`▱`); a retried stage is `合併（第 2 次）` / `Merge (try 2)` and the ladder does not grow. An app with no landed train yet, or a stage its last landed train did not pass (a rollback), shows the stage's name alone. Between two steps the last finished stage shows with `✓`. `since` is the start of the step in progress as its gate file records it, else the moment the sb server first saw the step in progress (a review runs no command); a waiting car's is its receipt; an ended car's the end.

Which todo a row goes under (the session decides; the server knows no todo): the open todos (pending or in progress) whose subject names one of the car's tickets as a word, case aside. One is that todo; with several, the first in progress, else the first by id; none puts the row in `Other cars` with its ticket in `wait`. An integrator's trains go in `Trains`, one row per train it received cars for: `🚂 1127l FE2 ▰▰▰▱▱▱▱ 合併`, its tickets in `wait`.

## Where the facts come from

The sb server joins what it already reads, `cars-rows` on its control socket (`{ cmd: "cars-rows", session }`, answered `{ ok: true, cars: CarFact[] }`, where `CarFact` is `src/train/carfacts.ts`'s and no contract):

- its book of cars (`carbook.ts`): each car's newest life, its coordinator and the integrator that received it, the end and its version or reason. After a restart the journal on disk (`trainboard/<project>/cars/`) stands in for a car the book has not seen, as the yard does;
- the trainboards and `yard.json` (`trainyard.ts`): which train a car rides, its steps, waits and gate times, the forming trains and their departure rule, and the ladder of the app.

The cars of the answer are those whose coordinator, or whose integrator, is the session asked for. They are the same facts the page draws (`yards.projects[].cars` of the net, this computer only), so `/boss cars`, the todo panel and the GUI say the same. The book's facts are read without the session's secret: the answer grants nothing, and its words are those the page already shows to anyone with the GUI.

A slip the session sent and no integrator has received yet is the session's own memory (`src/carrows.ts`, on `globalThis`, so `/reload` keeps it; never a file): every `call_tower` with `why: "shared"` that the tower took, for each slip in its message whose `coordinator:` is this session. The memory forgets a slip once the server holds a car of that head, and after a day.

## The reminder

When a slip in that memory is 15 minutes old and the server still holds no car of its head, the session's sb sends its model one message (`straw-boss-car-unreceived`, a follow-up that starts a turn): the ticket and head, and what to do (ask the integrator whether it has the slip, or send the slip with `call_tower` `why: "shared"` again so the tower forwards it again). Once per slip; never for a slip the server received, returned or dropped; nothing more after it.

## `/boss cars`

Prints the same rows, with their times, grouped under their todos (`#id subject`), `Other cars` and `Trains`, in sb's language. It needs no ledger and no GUI.

## The GUI

A main's unit card carries a small card per car of that main, from `yards.projects[].cars`, with the same words in the page's language: the ladder or the phase, the time, what it waits on, and `看這班火車 ›` / `See this train ›`, which opens the yard of its project scrolled to its train. Returned and dropped cards are red with the first line of the reason; a landed one shows its version; each goes 30 minutes after the end. Zen stays at the counts.
